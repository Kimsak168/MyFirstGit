About Us

Learn more about our journey and mission in the world of technology

Our Story

Founded in 2010, My Computer Shop has been delivering cutting-edge technology to enthusiasts, professionals, and businesses in Cambodia. From high-performance laptops to desktop computers and accessories, our goal is to provide the best products and services to our customers.

We believe in quality, reliability, and building long-term relationships with our clients. Our team is dedicated to providing expert advice and support to help you choose the right technology for your needs.

Our Journey

2010 - Founded

Started as a small computer retail shop in Phnom Penh, focusing on laptops and components.

2013 - Expansion

Expanded inventory to include gaming laptops, high-end desktops, and accessories.

2017 - Online Store Launch

Launched our e-commerce website to reach customers nationwide.

2022 - TechZone Rebranding

Modernized the brand and upgraded store facilities to provide a premium shopping experience.
